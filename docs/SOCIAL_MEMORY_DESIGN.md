# Social Memory & Relationship Gameplay

This pass turns friendship from a single score into a visible, recoverable social system.

## Relationship dimensions

Each recurring student tracks:

- **Friendship** — general closeness earned through repeated positive contact.
- **Trust** — follow-through, practical support, and promises kept.
- **Respect** — listening, fair competition, and taking the student seriously.
- **Strain** — hurt, embarrassment, broken promises, and unresolved tension.
- **Standing** — friendship + trust + respect − strain.

## Memories

The latest eight meaningful interactions are retained per student. Memories carry the school date and a concise description, allowing later conversations and the Social Notebook to reflect what actually happened.

## Promises

Players may make short-term promises during conversations. Open promises appear in the Social Notebook. Following through before the due date improves reliability, friendship, and trust. Ignoring the commitment marks it broken and creates strain.

## Recovery

Strained relationships are not permanently lost. A contextual reconciliation choice appears when strain is meaningful. Repair lowers strain and creates a new memory, but does not erase the history.

## Design intent

Failure should generate more story rather than simply closing content. A damaged friendship becomes a recovery route; a promise creates a small objective; a remembered act changes later dialogue. This makes daily conversations support long-term social strategy and emotional continuity.
